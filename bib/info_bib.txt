references.bib is a conflict magnet: everyone appends to the end of the same file.

Options, in order of preference:

1. Split by topic, mirroring the sections: bib/power-electronics.bib , bib/control.bib , bib/standards.bib .
Then \addbibresource{bib/power-electronics.bib} for each. Cheap and effective.

2. Keep the file sorted by citation key. Insertions then scatter, so two people appending different entries usually
land in different places.

3. One person owns the .bib . Others request entries via an issue. Works for small teams, creates a bottleneck.

Use consistent citation keys: author2023shorttitle , e.g. hodkiewicz2021maintenance . Export from Zotero or
JabRef rather than hand-writing entries — hand-written BibTeX is where inconsistent author formatting comes
from.
